<objective>
**As a** report engine service, **I want** to introspect all tables, columns, primary keys, and physical/virtual foreign keys from `TMS_Lite_1_Oct_2026` into an in-memory **Labeled Multigraph** (`D-03`, `D-04`, `D-05`), **so that** single-hop, multi-hop, composite (`14`), self-referencing (`5`), and role-playing multi-FK (`14` pairs such as `CL_Thc_Header` -> `CL_Master_Location`) relationships are represented with 100% fidelity.

Purpose: Implement `SchemaIntrospectionService` (`SCHEMA-02`) and `FkGraphService` (`SCHEMA-03`) with thread-safe in-memory caching and `ReloadGraphAsync()`.
Output: `SchemaModels.cs`, `SchemaIntrospectionService.cs`, `FkGraphService.cs`, and xUnit test suites `SchemaIntrospectionTests.cs` & `FkGraphServiceTests.cs`.
</objective>
